Privacy

What I process, what I keep, for how long, and how to have it deleted.

Updated October 30, 2026.

Who is responsible

Medido. You can reach me at marketingmedido@gmail.com.

What the reading processes

The web address you paste. I read only the home page and ignore anything after the address. To limit repeated readings, your IP address is held in memory in a scrambled form that changes every day; it is not written to disk.

What I keep

For 30 days, a short technical record of the site that was read (up to 10 per site): tracking IDs, event counts, the domain's expiry date, whether robots and sitemap exist, and a scrambled version of the title. The full report is kept for 6 hours, so you can see it again if you paste the same site; then it is deleted. With no time limit, so that each site gets only one free reading and for your Second reading coupon: the site's address (only the domain), the date and language of its readings, the coupon (its code, when it was issued, when it expires and whether it was used), the PayPal order number if you paid, and what was done with the reading and when (whether it was copied or shared, the coupon saved to a calendar, or a payment started). I never keep your name, your email or your IP address. For 90 days, daily usage totals (for example, how many readings there were), with no site and no visitor data. If you answer the "Is this correct?" question, I keep your answer for 90 days with the reading it refers to.

Sites I read

My readings are real requests. They can show up in the analytics and ad accounts of the site I read, as visits and contact events from a Chrome browser whose user agent ends in MedidoLupa/0.2. The reader honors a robots.txt rule that disallows MedidoLupa.

If you write to me

If you use the contact form, what you type (your email, your site address, a spend range if you pick one, and your message) goes to Formspree, a form service that passes it to my inbox and, on the plan I use today, keeps a copy in its archive for 30 days. I use it only to answer you. I keep our messages for as long as we are in touch, and for as long as the law requires. I send the free reading only if you ask for it.

If you connect Google

Connecting is optional and is only offered to clients who have ordered the Diagnosis. The free reading never needs it. If you connect, you sign in on Google's own page and allow read-only access to Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager (the permissions analytics.readonly and tagmanager.readonly). I never see your Google password. The access cannot change, delete or publish anything. The page where you connect is /connect/; I send that link only to clients.

I use what the access shows only to prepare your written Diagnosis report, within 10 business days. A person at Medido reads that data: you agree to this by ticking the box before you connect, as Google requires. I look at the setup of your accounts and at totals, not at data about individual people.

What I keep from your Google connection

The sign-in key that Google gives me (a refresh token) is stored encrypted, together with the address of your site, the date the connection was made and the date it ends. I don't store your Google account email. The short-lived access keys live only in the server's memory and are never written to disk. A connection lasts 14 days. When it ends, I revoke it at Google and delete it automatically; a check runs every hour. I also keep a short access log (date, site address, and whether the connection was made, revoked or expired), with no keys and no Google data, until you ask me to delete it.

What I look at is read from Google while I review it. The only things I keep from the review are my working notes and the report I write for you, and I delete them if you ask.

Disconnecting and revoking

Press Disconnect on the connection page and I revoke the access at Google and delete the connection at once. You can also remove it yourself at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions: the connection stops working immediately, and I delete my copy the next time I try to use it or when it expires. Or write to me at marketingmedido@gmail.com and I'll do it. If Google doesn't confirm a revocation, I retry every hour for 24 hours and then delete my copy anyway; you can always remove the access from the permissions page.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Medido's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In plain words: I use Google data only to give you the report you ordered. I don't sell it, I don't use it for advertising, and I don't pass it to anyone else, except where the law requires; the hosting service that runs this site only holds the encrypted connection. A person reads it only because you agreed.

Third parties and cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own unless you connect your Google account. In that case it sets two technical cookies, needed only for the sign-in and to recognize your connection until it ends; they are not used for tracking. It loads its fonts from its own server. The contact form is processed by Formspree, as explained above. If I turn on visit measurement with Google Analytics or the Meta pixel (to measure my own ads), a notice asks you first and it loads only if you accept; then Google or Meta set cookies to measure visits and contact requests. The same notice covers Microsoft Clarity, which records how the pages are used (clicks, scrolling and session replays with the text you type masked) and sets its own cookies only if you accept. If you decline, nothing loads. If I turn on a bot check, the provider will receive your IP address and browser details for that check only. The site is served by a hosting provider that sees the requests it handles. In your browser I keep only the site, your coupon code and its expiry date, so you don't have to type it again. It isn't a measurement cookie, and it's deleted when you use the coupon or when it expires.

Deleting your data

Write to me and I'll delete the record of your domain and its subdomains. I can also exclude your domain from future readings. I may ask you to write from an address at that domain. If you connected Google, I also delete the connection, the access log lines and my notes on it. For your site's address and your coupon, write to me through the contact form and we'll sort it out together.

Payments: Second reading and sessions

The Second reading (US$5 with your coupon) is paid on PayPal's own page: I never see your card or account details; I keep only the order number and the date of the payment. If Lupa can't do your Second reading, I refund the full US$5. Once the reading is done, there's no refund.

The 45-minute reading session is booked through Cal.com and paid through PayPal. You can reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before the session. If you cancel more than 24 hours before, you get a full refund. If you cancel with less notice or do not show up, there is no refund, and I offer a new date once. If I cancel, you choose a full refund or a new date. Once the session has taken place there is no refund; its price is credited against the Diagnosis if you continue within 14 days. To request a refund, reply to your confirmation email; the refund is issued through PayPal within a few business days.
