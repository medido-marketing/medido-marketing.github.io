The Diagnosis

The Diagnosis: a GA4 and Tag Manager audit with a verdict on your agency

A verdict first, then the proof

The Diagnosis by Medido (marketingmedido.com) is a one-time, flat-fee audit of your marketing measurement: a snapshot of your last quarter, delivered in 10 business days as a written report and a 45-minute call, for US$925. Page 1 is a verdict on your agency or provider: Keep, Change or Stop.

The free reading stops at your front door. The Diagnosis goes inside: with your permission I read your Google Analytics and Tag Manager, and Search Console if you allow it, read-only, and give you a written report with a verdict: Keep, Change or Stop.

What the written report covers

Page 1 is the verdict on your agency or provider: Keep, Change or Stop. Then it covers five parts:

What you paid and what you got

Channel by channel, last quarter, with the method shown.

What is measured wrong, and what it costs you

Double counting, a pixel firing in a loop, leads that never show up in the numbers.

Where the budget is misplaced

Money going to channels or audiences that can't bring a customer.

Who owns what

Ads, analytics, domain, social: what is at risk if you change providers tomorrow.

What to ask for

Specific requests for your team or agency, with a way to check that each one was done.

What I need from you

Once the first half is paid, I send you the link to Connect Google: a normal Google sign-in that gives me read-only access to your Google Analytics, Tag Manager and, if you allow it, Search Console, for up to 14 days. No passwords, and it can't change anything. Connect doesn't read Google Ads or Meta: I judge those from outside and from your agency's reports. Your accounts stay in your name.

Price and delivery

US$925 · written verdict in 10 business days

Flat fee, never a percentage of your ad spend. Half to start and half on delivery, paid by PayPal. In 10 business days you get the written report, with the verdict on page 1, and a 45-minute call. It includes a Second reading after you make the fixes. It makes sense from about US$1k a month in ad spend. If you had a Reading session, it is fully credited if you continue within 14 days.
