Before you connect, read this

What it gets into
Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, read-only. It can't change, delete or publish anything. Google's own sign-in screen shows these two permissions. You sign in on Google's page and I never see your password.
What I look at
How your measurement is set up and what it counts: your Analytics accounts, properties and data streams, the events and conversions you have set up and their daily totals, and in Tag Manager the containers, tags, triggers and variables.
What I don't look at
Anything about individual people. I look at totals and settings, never at who your visitors or customers are.
Who reads it
A person at Medido reads this data. Google asks for your clear agreement before a person reads data from your account, so the box below is required.
How long
14 days. After that the access is revoked at Google and deleted on my side, on its own.
