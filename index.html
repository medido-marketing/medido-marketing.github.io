When not to hire me

Four cases where the honest answer is no

  • Your agency or team already shows you numbers you can check. Then you don't need me.
  • You want someone to run your ads. I don't, but I have people I trust and we can team up.
  • You can't give me read-only access to your accounts because your agency won't hand over the access.
  • You want a quick call or a WhatsApp answer. I work in writing, on purpose: it leaves a record of what we do and what we say.

For your agency

Five questions to send to your agency today

No need to accuse anyone. A good agency answers these in an hour. How they answer is information.

  1. Who administers our Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Google Ads and Meta accounts? Can you give me read-only access?
  2. Which actions count as a conversion, and how is each one defined?
  3. Is any event counted twice? How did you check?
  4. What did each channel cost per real inquiry last quarter, and how do you define real?
  5. Who owns the domain and the ad accounts, and what happens to them if we stop working together?

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you run my ads?
No. Flat fee, never a percentage of your spend, so my income doesn't grow when your spend grows. I also sell follow-up services, so weigh my verdict by the evidence I show.
Who owns the accounts?
You, from day one. Ads, analytics, domain and social accounts stay in your name; you can leave whenever you want and lose nothing.
Is the work subcontracted?
No. I do the work. I use software to read and draft, I check what I send, and I am responsible for it.
What do you keep of my site?
Not the page. For 30 days, a short technical record (tracking IDs, event counts, the domain's expiry) so I can tell you what changed, and for 90 days a usage counter with a scrambled domain. Ask me to delete it any time. My reader identifies itself as MedidoLupa and honors a robots.txt rule for it; or write to me.
What if everything is fine?
Then I say so. The Diagnosis costs the same whether the verdict is Keep, Change or Stop. I also sell follow-up services, so weigh my verdict by the evidence.
Why in writing?
Because it leaves a record you can forward to your agency or your accountant, and you aren't tied to my calendar.
What do you need from me?
For the reading, only your website. For the Diagnosis, read-only access to your accounts (no passwords) and 30 minutes at the start.
